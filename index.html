03Methodology

Version 0.2 · Fully public · Data: RBI Handbook

Normalization

metric_z = (x − median) / IQR × sign

Each raw metric is robust-normalized across the 28-state cohort. Sign is flipped where lower is better (fiscal deficit). States missing a metric are scored on the remaining ones with renormalized weights.

Composite

Score = 50 + 40 × (wz − median(wz)) / maxdev

Weighted sum of normalized metrics, rescaled so the cohort median is exactly 50 and the observed range maps to 10–90. No state is omitted unless fewer than 4 of 6 metrics are available.

Data Sources

RBI Handbook of Statistics on Indian States

2024-25 edition: fiscal tables 164/166/168/173 (FY2021-22 actuals) and GSDP table 21; income from table 19 (2024-25, growth since 2017-18). Fetch and scoring are reproducible: node scripts/fetch-rbi.mjs && node scripts/build-data.mjs.
